SUPPORT POLICY HANDBOOK
Internal reference for the support agent. Do not quote this document to a customer.

SECTION 1 — Refunds and returns
1.1 A refund request is valid when the order number and the delivery date are both present in the ticket.
1.2 Damaged goods must be reported within fourteen days of delivery, and the original packaging stays with the customer until the claim closes.
1.3 A refund is issued to the original payment method unless the customer asks for store credit in writing.
1.4 Partial refunds require the amount to be stated as a number, not as a percentage range.
1.5 Refunds above two hundred are reviewed by a supervisor before they are released.
1.6 A refund that is already in processing cannot be cancelled, only reversed after it settles.
1.7 Store credit expires after twelve months and cannot be extended by an agent.
1.8 The refund reason must be recorded as one of the four allowed codes.

SECTION 2 — Shipping and delivery
2.1 An order is considered late when it is more than three working days past the promised date.
2.2 The promised date is the one shown at checkout, not the estimate in the confirmation email.
2.3 Missing parcels are traced with the carrier before a replacement is sent.
2.4 A replacement for a missing parcel is sent only after the carrier closes the trace.
2.5 Address changes are possible until the parcel is handed to the carrier.
2.6 Delivery to a pickup point counts as delivered on the day it arrives there.
2.7 Customs delays are outside the delivery promise and are explained to the customer as such.
2.8 Shipping fees are refunded only when the delay was caused by the warehouse.

SECTION 3 — Warranty and repairs
3.1 The warranty covers manufacturing defects and does not cover damage from liquid or from a drop.
3.2 A repair ticket needs a photograph of the defect before it can be queued.
3.3 Repairs take between ten and twenty working days from the day the parcel arrives at the workshop.
3.4 The customer pays shipping to the workshop and the company pays the return shipping.
3.5 A device that cannot be repaired is replaced with the same model or with the next model up.
3.6 Warranty claims do not extend the warranty period.
3.7 Proof of purchase can be a receipt, an invoice, or the order number in this system.
3.8 A serial number that does not match the order is escalated before any repair is approved.

SECTION 4 — Escalation and tone
4.1 An escalation is opened when the customer asks for a supervisor twice in the same ticket.
4.2 Every escalation must state what was already tried and what the customer wants.
4.3 The agent never promises a date that the warehouse has not confirmed.
4.4 Apologies are short and followed by the next concrete step.
4.5 A customer who is angry about a delay is offered a tracking update first, not a discount.
4.6 Discounts above ten percent require a supervisor code.
4.7 A ticket that waits for the customer is closed after seven days without a reply.
4.8 Every closed ticket records the resolution code and the amount refunded, including zero.
